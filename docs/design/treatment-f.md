# Treatment: home, version F ("black stage, echoes of the gallop")

Design exploration, not a build. The artboard lives in the owner's design
canvas next to versions B to E. Method: `frontend-design`, `cinematic-ui`
(guidelines edition) and `awwwards-ui-design`.

## Development

- Product truth: a personal portfolio. The owner is a creative coder who
  directs a bespoke studio; client work lives on the studio's site. The one
  action that matters: write to him.
- Logline: a visitor expects one more web designer, meets still images that
  start moving when code touches them, and leaves knowing this person draws
  with code.
- Audience arc: arrives "another portfolio"; leaves "he makes pictures move,
  and he let me try".

## Casting session

Written from memory. Nothing here was researched or checked against frames;
every line is [I] and must be verified before it is treated as fact.

| Candidate | Mechanism | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Norman McLaren, Pas de deux | A lit figure on black whose earlier positions stay on screen as echoes, then fold back into one | Can read as a visual effect with no content | Kept |
| Chris Marker, La Jetee | A film of stills where movement happens once | Needs photographs and a narration the owner has not written | Set aside |
| Saul Bass, title sequences | Flat shape and type carry the frame | Already cast by the studio's own site | Set aside |

Mechanisms taken, each visible on screen:

1. Black stage: the hero has no ground, only the figure and unlit dots.
2. Echoes: up to eight earlier frames of the gallop stay behind the horse,
   smaller and in the accent colour.
3. The echoes separate: under the hero, the same frames stand apart as a strip.
4. Hold: the last image is one frame, no echo, no motion.
5. One accent with one meaning: blue is time already past (echoes, the active
   item, the single action).
6. The visitor controls time: a slider sets how many echoes follow the horse.

## Plan

- Colour: stage black `#000000`, paper `#ededeb`, ink `#141414`, signal
  `#0b14c9` on paper and `#2a36ff` on black, quiet `#4d4d49`.
- Type: one family for everything (Host Grotesk, light, lowercase per the
  owner); a mono face only inside the notes receipt and the clock.
- Layout: black stage, paper document, black ending. Left aligned throughout.
- Lead: the horse. States: gallops with echoes; stands apart as frames; is
  covered by travelling cells; becomes halftone in the lab; stops in one frame.
- Audience's part: the echoes slider (pointer, touch and keyboard).

## Revisions against the plan

- Removed the arrow glyph on links and buttons and the pixel display face.
- Replaced the six-cell lab grid with a list in large type.
- Dropped the "frames" caption over the strip at the owner's request.
